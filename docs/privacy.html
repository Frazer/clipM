clip'M

Privacy Policy

5 October 2026

clip'M is a clipboard manager for Mac, developed by United Visions. What you copy stays on your Mac. We do not run an account system, and we do not collect your clipboard.

What stays on your Mac

clip'M saves clipboard history, snippets, and action scripts in ~/Library/Application Support/ClipM on that computer. History can include text, links, file names, and images you copied. Preferences, such as keyboard shortcuts, stay on the Mac as well.

That information is not sent to United Visions. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Permissions

clip'M asks for Accessibility, and for permission to send Apple Events, so it can paste the clip you choose into the app you are using. Those permissions are used for paste. They are not used to watch what you do in other apps.

Update checks

If you installed clip'M from the Mac App Store and check for an update, the app asks Apple whether a newer version is listed. That request includes the app’s bundle identifier. It does not include your clipboard.

If you installed the direct download, clip'M uses Sparkle to look for a new release on GitHub. That check does not include your clipboard.

Deleting your information

Clear history from inside clip'M, or delete ~/Library/Application Support/ClipM after you quit the app. Removing the app does not by itself remove that folder.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to github.com/Frazer/clipM or unitedvisions.org.
